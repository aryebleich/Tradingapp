4 independent sleeves × 25% · Every trading day
Four separate models each run a quarter of the money: Nasdaq trend (TQQQ/SQQQ), semiconductors (SOXL), a 202-day trend vote across SPY/QQQ/IWM (TECL or T-bills), and S&P trend (SPXL or anti-beta).
Holds one leveraged ETF at 100% · Every trading day
Bull market (SPY above its 200-day average): TQQQ unless Nasdaq or the S&P is overbought, then volatility (UVXY). Bear market: buys oversold dips in TECL / SPXL, otherwise rotates between TQQQ, SQQQ, TECS and short-term bonds.
Steps 1-3 are exactly as in the sheet. The sheet export did not include the rules for steps 4-12, so they are rebuilt from the well-known "TQQQ For The Long Term" logic the sheet's step list matches. Check them against your code and adjust.
Gold-miner fade and Nasdaq momentum tree · Every trading day
Starts with the GDXU RSI exhaustion gate and the Nasdaq 90-day vs 70-day momentum split, then a long tree of RSI, trend and return checks across leveraged ETFs and single stocks.
The sheet export only shows the rules for the steps on that day's path (steps 1, 2 and 4). The other ~105 steps have no thresholds in the export, so the walk stops with "needs a rule" when it reaches one. Send the QC #687 code (or the sheet itself) to fill them in, or type them in the editor.
One stock: the strongest 12-month momentum · Weekly
Every Wednesday ranks 50 large US companies by their 252-day return and holds the single strongest one at 100%. The original code uses a 10% stop.
The sheet builds the list from price > $5, top 100 by dollar volume and top 50 by market cap. Here the list is the sheet's current top 50 and you can edit it.
Buy the dip in an uptrend, sell the bounce · Every trading day
Buys top Nasdaq-100 stocks in a long-term uptrend that dipped below the lower Bollinger band with weak RSI and a real pullback. Sells on the bounce back to the 15-day average, a break of the 200-day average, or after 15 days. Equal weight, up to 10 stocks.
Stocks, gold, bonds and bitcoin by predicted return · Monthly
Once a month, weights SPY, GLD, BND and bitcoin (IBIT) by their predicted 21-day return. Only positive predictions get money, bitcoin is capped, and the total never exceeds 100% (no margin).
In the sheet you type the model's 4 predictions each month. Here you can keep typing them (manual), or let the app use each asset's recent 3-month return as a simple stand-in (automatic).

SPY trend strategy
Two stocks that usually move together. When the gap between them stretches far from normal, sell the expensive one and buy the cheap one, then close when the gap returns.
Pairs that moved together in the past can stop doing so (a merger, a lawsuit, a new product). The stop level and the time limit exist for that. Short selling needs a margin account and has borrowing costs; the paper account trades the long-only version. Backtests are hypothetical.
Projections resample real 12-month stretches of S&P 500 history (crashes included) 2,000 times. They are not a forecast or a promise; the market can fall for years.
