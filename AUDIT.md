# Part 1: layout v2 inventory and audit

Phase 1 changed no code. The inventory was measured by `test/layout/run.mjs` on the layout that is live today ("old"): every page at 1440, 1280, 768 and 390px, with sandbox data (Alpaca mock). The table was generated by `test/layout/audit.mjs`. Baseline files are in `e2e-output/layout-baseline/` (not in git; screenshots), with a summary in LAYOUT_TEST_RESULTS.md.

## 1. Routes and pages (122)

- **Public site (10):** `/` (homepage), `/yi` (Yiddish homepage), `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/risk`, `/contact`, `/status`, `/security`, `/insurance`, and the 404 page.
- **Signed-out app screens (4 measured, 7 in all):** `#/login`, `#/register`, `#/forgot`, `#/login-phone`. Also `#/reset`, `#/verify` and `#/mfa`, which open only from an e-mail link or a sign-in in progress; they use the same card layout as `#/login`.
- **Signed-in app pages (108):** every page in the menu, which is what an owner sees, so admin pages are included. They are listed in the table in section 3. Some routes are aliases of these (`#/billing` = `#/funding`, `#/strategy?id=…` = detail of `#/strategies`, `#/hotline` = `#/text?mode=phone`); they share a layout and were covered by the end-to-end run.
