## Phase 2: proposed page structure

1. **Introduction**
   1. Header (sticky). Unchanged role; becomes sticky so the CTA and risk link stay reachable.
   2. Hero. Fewer competing actions: one primary CTA ("Start free with practice money") and one secondary (demo).
   3. How it works. Moved up so the 3-step promise answers "how hard is this?" right after the hero.
2. **Products and Features**
   1. Two ways to grow (merged with "Why different"). The two pages say the same thing from two angles; one section is shorter.
   2. Strategies and Reality Check (merged). Reality Check is the reason to trust the strategies, so they belong together.
   3. Live demo. Moved next to strategies so visitors can try what they just read about.
   4. Features (tabbed: Trading, Investing, Automation, AI), including the assistant/phone section. Tabs turn the longest section into four short ones.
   5. Ad video. Moved to support Products rather than interrupt the hero.
3. **Long-term investing**
   1. S&P 500 plan and calculator. Kept as its own block because it is the main investing path and carries its own assumptions.
4. **Pricing and Fees**
   1. Pricing and credits. Unchanged position, after the value is shown.
5. **Education and Support**
   1. New to investing? Start here. Moved here, with the Learning center, so beginners find one learning place.
   2. FAQ. Moved next to Education because both answer questions before sign-up.
6. **Trust and Compliance**
   1. You stay in control (security, your own broker), plus a new "Risks and how we are regulated" block with links to Risk, Terms, Privacy, Security and the broker's disclosures. This groups every trust signal and disclosure in one reachable place.
7. **Action**
   1. Final CTA, with a link to the risk disclosure beside the button. This keeps the risk reachable from the last CTA, as from every CTA.
8. **Footer**
   1. Legal links, the full disclaimer (unchanged), and the regulatory line [PLACEHOLDER: verified value]. Unchanged role.

## Phase 4: implementation summary

**Group: Homepage v2 (R1 order, R2 one main button, R3 risk links, R4 Trust & compliance)**, one flag `home_v2`, default **off**, rollout 0 to 100% (Admin → SEO → Feature flags).
- Files: `scripts/home.py` (`regroup()` builds v2 from the same sections), `scripts/pages.py` (writes `index-v2.html`, `yi-v2.html`), `scripts/yi_home.py` (Yiddish), `public/site.css`, `public/site.js` (button-click counter), `src/flags.js`, `server.js` (variant per visitor, `Cache-Control: private` and `Vary: Cookie` only while the flag is on; no cookie while it is off), `src/analytics.js` (`count('cta', name)`), `public/pages/admin-ops.js` (flags card).
- Content: every v1 sentence is still on v2 except the three hero sign-up buttons, which become one main button plus smaller Google and GitHub buttons. The Trust & compliance block repeats the existing footer disclosure word for word.
- Tests: `test/homev2.test.js` (flag default and rollout, all sections and disclosures kept, accessibility basics, server variant and rollback, admin-only endpoints). Visual check with Playwright at 1280 and 390 px, English and Yiddish: no horizontal overflow.
- Sign-off needed: LEGAL_REVIEW.md L9 to L12, before the flag is turned on.
- Rollback: switch the flag off (takes effect on the next page load), or revert the commit.
