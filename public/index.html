Popular questions

Can I turn my TradingView alerts into trades?

Yes. Create a webhook under Alerts to trades, paste its URL into the alert’s Webhook URL field, and use our message template. Strategy alerts can pass the direction and size with {{strategy.order.action}} and {{strategy.order.contracts}}.

Can I run one strategy across all my accounts?

One webhook can send each alert to your paper account and your connected Alpaca account at the same time, with the same risk rules. Each QuantPocket login connects one Alpaca account.

I pick my own entries. Can the rest be automated?

Yes. Enter with a bracket order and the stop-loss and take-profit are placed with it. You can also send exit alerts or schedule trades.

Can my AI agent place real trades?

Any program that can send an HTTPS request can use a webhook, AI agents included. It can only do what that webhook allows: its accounts, sizes and risk rules. Start on paper.

Can I send my QuantConnect algorithm to my broker?

You can create QuantConnect projects and run cloud backtests from QuantPocket. For live orders, QuantConnect can trade your Alpaca account directly, or your algorithm can call a QuantPocket webhook so its orders follow your risk rules here.

Can I set a swing trade and walk away?

Yes. Place a bracket order (entry, stop-loss and take-profit) as good-till-canceled. The exit orders wait at Alpaca even while you are offline.

Can I automate trades on my own brokerage account?

Yes, on your own Alpaca account. Live trading and sending alerts to Alpaca are part of Pro, which you can try free for 14 days.

Can my strategy run fully hands-off?

Bots run your rules every trading day and webhooks act on alerts around the clock. Automation can still fail or act on bad data, so check your account regularly.

Can I send TrendSpider bot alerts to my broker?

Yes. TrendSpider bots can call a webhook URL. Use your QuantPocket webhook URL and the same JSON message.

Can I trigger trades from Zapier?

Yes. Add a Webhooks by Zapier POST step with your webhook URL and the fields ticker, action and quantity.

Will I make money?

Nobody can promise that. Most active strategies underperform simply holding an index after costs. QuantPocket shows how an idea behaved in the past, how it held up out of sample, and the range of outcomes to expect, so you can decide with your eyes open.

Can I sign up with Google?

Yes. Choose Sign up with Google and your account is ready in seconds, with a confirmed email. You can add a password later in Settings.

Who holds my money?

Your brokerage account is opened and held by Alpaca Securities LLC, a FINRA/SIPC member. QuantPocket connects to it with access you control and can revoke at any time.

How does the free trial work?

Every paid plan starts with 14 days free. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. The free Starter plan has no time limit.

How does the performance fee work?

Each month we compare your account to its previous peak, adjusted for deposits and withdrawals. You pay the plan’s percentage only on value above that peak. Losses must be recovered before any new fee is charged.

Is texting trades secure?

Commands only work from the phone number you verified, and every order needs your personal PIN. You can pause all bots and scheduled trades with one text.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from Manage billing in the app; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. You can also export or delete all your data from Settings.
