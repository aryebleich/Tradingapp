Popular questions

Can I turn my chart alerts into trades?

Yes. Create a webhook under Alerts to trades, paste its URL into the alert’s Webhook URL field, and use our message template. Strategy alerts can pass the direction and size with {{strategy.order.action}} and {{strategy.order.contracts}}.

Where do the ready-made strategies come from?

They are rule-based rotation strategies with recorded backtests, the same rules for everyone. We show each backtest next to its live results, and Reality Check keeps watching them for you. A great backtest is not a promise: several of them did much worse live.

What is Reality Check?

Every trading day we compare a strategy’s live result with its backtest. If they drift apart, you get a text or email, and with Auto-throttle on, the money that strategy trades is cut to 50% (Watch) or 0% (Drifting) until it recovers.

Can I run one strategy across all my accounts?

Yes. Webhooks can send each alert to several accounts at once (paper, Alpaca, Schwab, tastytrade, Tradier, TradeStation), with the same risk rules.

Can my AI agent place real trades?

Any program that can send an HTTPS request can use a webhook, AI agents included. It can only do what that webhook allows: its accounts, sizes and risk rules. Start on paper.

Can I set a swing trade and walk away?

Yes. Place a bracket order (entry, stop-loss and take-profit) as good-till-canceled. The exit orders wait at your broker even while you are offline.

Can my strategy run fully hands-off?

Automatic strategies, pair bots and webhooks run without you. Automation can still fail or act on bad data, so check your account regularly.

What happens if my broker or market data has a problem?

If a broker keeps failing, orders to it pause for a short cool-down instead of piling up, and you see the error in the app. Market data can be delayed or missing at times, so keep stop-loss orders at your broker for anything important.

Can I trigger trades from automation tools?

Yes. Any tool that can call a webhook URL works. Use your Brightfolio webhook URL with the fields ticker, action and quantity.

Will I make money?

Nobody can promise that. Most active strategies underperform simply holding an index after costs. Brightfolio shows how an idea behaved in the past, how it is doing live, and the range of outcomes to expect, so you can decide with your eyes open.

Can I try it without linking a broker?

Yes. Every account starts with a $100,000 paper account using real market prices. You can test strategies, webhooks and the assistant without connecting anything.

Are my broker keys and password safe?

Passwords are hashed, broker keys are encrypted with AES-256, and keys are only used on our servers. You can add two-factor sign-in or a passkey, and lock the account with one tap.

Is Brightfolio for beginners or experienced traders?

Both. Beginners can practice on paper and follow ready-made plans like the S&P 500 autopilot. Experienced traders get webhooks, pairs trading, options, the code lab and automation across several brokers.

Can I use Brightfolio in Yiddish?

Yes. In the app, open Settings → Language and choose אידיש (full translation). The whole app switches to Yiddish, written right-to-left.

Which brokerages can I connect?

Alpaca (you can also open a new account inside Brightfolio), Charles Schwab, tastytrade, Tradier and TradeStation. Your money always stays at your broker.

Can I sign up with Google?

Yes. Choose Sign up with Google and your account is ready in seconds. You can add a password later in Settings.

Can I trade with real money on the free plan?

Yes. Connect your own brokerage account and place your own orders on any plan, including Starter. Automatic strategies, bots and webhooks on real accounts are part of the paid plans.

What are credits?

Every plan includes credits each month (100 on Starter up to 10,000 on Ultimate). An extra AI message or a text alert costs 1 credit, a phone call 3, a filing summary 5 and training an AI agent 10. Monthly credits refill on the 1st of the month; credit packs you buy (from $5) never expire.

How does the free trial work?

Every paid plan starts with 14 days free. Cancel before the trial ends and you are not charged. The free Starter plan has no time limit.

How does the performance fee work?

Each month we compare your account to its previous peak, adjusted for deposits and withdrawals. You pay the plan’s percentage only on value above that peak. Losses must be recovered before any new fee is charged.

Is texting trades secure?

Commands only work from the phone number you verified, every order needs your personal PIN, and phone calls read the order back before it is placed.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from Manage billing in the app; you keep access until the end of the period you paid for. You can also export or delete all your data from Settings.
