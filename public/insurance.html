Insurance and protection

Last updated October 22, 2026

Brightfolio never holds your money or your investments. They stay in accounts in your own name, at firms with their own protection.

Your brokerage account

Stocks and cash are held by your broker, for example Alpaca Securities LLC, a member of FINRA and SIPC. If a SIPC-member brokerage fails, SIPC protects the securities and cash in your account up to $500,000, of which up to $250,000 can be cash.

SIPC does not protect against investment losses when prices fall, and it does not cover crypto. Ask your broker whether it carries extra ("excess SIPC") coverage.

Your bank accounts

Banks you link stay at your bank. Deposits at an FDIC-insured bank are insured up to $250,000 per depositor, per bank, for each account ownership category. Linking a bank to Brightfolio is read-only and does not change that.

Crypto

Crypto is not covered by SIPC or FDIC. Wallets you add to Brightfolio are watched by public address only; Brightfolio never holds crypto or keys.
