Privacy Policy

Last updated October 22, 2026

This policy explains what personal information QuantPocket collects, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account data: email address, a hashed password, optional phone number, a hashed PIN, two-factor settings, and tax-rate preferences. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your email address and an account identifier with us; we do not receive your Google password.
  • Webhooks: the alerts you send to your webhook URLs and the resulting orders, kept in your signal log.
  • Brokerage application data: when you open a brokerage account we collect your legal name, date of birth, Social Security number, citizenship, address, phone, employment and regulatory disclosures. This is sent directly to Alpaca Securities LLC for identity verification (KYC) and account opening. QuantPocket does not store your Social Security number; we keep only your Alpaca account ID, account number and status.
  • Bank details: bank routing and account numbers you link for transfers are sent to Alpaca; we do not store them.
  • Trading data: strategies, paper and synced live orders, positions, scheduled trades, alerts and the ledger of fees and deposits.
  • Connected services: brokerage and QuantConnect keys and access tokens, encrypted with AES-256-GCM.
  • Messages to the assistant: texts and spoken requests you send to our phone number, used to answer you and kept in your security log.
  • Security data: IP address, browser type and a log of sign-ins and sensitive actions.
  • Payments: handled by Stripe. We never see or store full card numbers.

How we use it

To provide the Service, open and maintain your brokerage account with Alpaca, secure your account, process payments and fees, send messages you request (such as SMS replies, alerts and password resets), comply with law, and improve the product. We do not sell personal information.

Who we share it with

Service providers that run parts of the Service on our behalf: hosting and database (Render, Turso), brokerages you choose to connect (Alpaca, Charles Schwab, tastytrade, Tradier, TradeStation), research (QuantConnect), messaging (Twilio, Resend), payments (Stripe, Helcim), sign-in (Google, if you use it) and AI (Anthropic: for the strategy builder, and for messages you send to the text and phone assistant). We may disclose information when required by law.

Cookies

We use one essential, HttpOnly session cookie to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Retention

We keep your data while your account is open. When you delete your account we delete your data from our systems, except where we must keep records by law.

Your choices

From Settings you can download all your data (export) or permanently delete your account. You can also disconnect integrations and stop SMS at any time by replying STOP.

Security

Passwords and PINs are hashed with scrypt, API keys are encrypted, connections use HTTPS, and two-factor authentication is available. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a unique password and enable two-factor authentication.

Children

The Service is not for anyone under 18.

Contact

Privacy questions or requests: use the contact page.
