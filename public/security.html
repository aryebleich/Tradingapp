How your account is protected

  • Passwords are stored only as a scrypt hash; nobody at Brightfolio can read them.
  • Two-step sign-in with an authenticator app, or a passkey (Face ID, fingerprint or a security key) instead of a password.
  • Broker keys and bank links are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before they are saved.
  • Bank and card links are read-only (through Plaid): we see balances and transactions, never your bank password, and cannot move money. Crypto wallets are added by public address only: never keys or seed words.
  • Account lock stops all orders and withdrawals at once; text PAUSE with your PIN to stop every bot from your phone.
  • Active sessions: see every signed-in device and sign the others out.
  • Tamper-evident audit log: every order and sign-in is recorded in a hash-chained log, so a changed entry shows.
  • Nothing big happens silently: large orders ask you to confirm first, and trades from rules across accounts wait for your approval.

How the service is run

  • All traffic is encrypted (HTTPS only), with protection against cross-site request forgery and rate limits on sign-in.
  • Every change to the code is tested automatically before it goes live, with automatic dependency updates and a weekly security scan.
  • Order handling keeps working through broker outages: orders wait in a queue and are retried safely, without placing them twice.
