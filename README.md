## What is Brightolio?

Brightolio is investing and trading software, not a broker. It connects to your own brokerage account and gives you the tools professional traders use, explained so a beginner can follow:

1. **Practice** with pretend money at real market prices.
2. **Test** a strategy on past data before risking anything (backtesting).
3. **Automate** it with bots, scheduled trades and alerts, on paper first, then on your real account when you are ready.
4. **Invest for the long term** with low-cost index funds, an automatic S&P 500 plan and retirement planning.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Brightolio a broker?**
No. Brightolio is software. Your money and your trades stay at your own brokerage account (for example Alpaca, Charles Schwab, tastytrade, Tradier or TradeStation).

**Can I practice trading without real money?**
Yes. Every account starts with a free $100,000 paper trading account that uses real market prices.

**What is backtesting?**
Backtesting replays a trading strategy over past prices to show how it would have done: the return, the worst drop (max drawdown) and how often it won. Results are hypothetical and do not promise future returns.

**Can I automate trading without coding?**
Yes. Pick a strategy from the library, backtest it, and run it as a bot on paper or on your real account. Alerts from chart services can also place trades.

**Is there an app?**
Yes. Brightolio works in any browser on a phone or computer, and there is an Android app.

**Does Brightolio give investment advice?**
No. Brightolio provides tools and education, not personalized investment advice, and nothing on it is a recommendation to buy or sell.

**Is my account secure?**
Brightolio supports two-factor sign-in and passkeys, encrypts the keys you connect, and can lock your account (orders and withdrawals) with one tap.
