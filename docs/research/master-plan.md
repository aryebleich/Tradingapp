Do not try to build everything simultaneously. Establish the underlying architecture so that every new division can integrate with the rest of Brightolio.

Enterprise software, hedge fund tools, white-label licensing and international markets.

Phase 6 — Financial infrastructure

Explore regulated brokerage operations, institutional execution and additional financial services if commercially justified.

### Important legal and business considerations

You do not need to become a brokerage company immediately. Starting as a financial software and analytics company is a more manageable approach.

Executing securities transactions for customers, collecting transaction-based compensation, providing certain personalized investment services and operating automated or copy-trading services may trigger registration requirements. The SEC and FINRA specifically address these activities. Guide to Broker-Dealer Registration +2

You should also secure commercial market data licenses before distributing protected financial data to paying customers.
